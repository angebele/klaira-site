Before booking a meeting

Questions we're often asked.

How long does an engagement last?
It depends on the diagnosis. A Consulting engagement lasts 4 to 8 weeks. A C-Level Advisory commits to 6 months minimum, renewable. Klaira plays the long game — not speed.
What does the first meeting look like?
45 minutes, no obligation. You talk. Fabien listens, asks the useful questions, and tells you honestly whether the engagement makes sense — or not.
Is Klaira right for me?
Klaira primarily supports leaders of established SMEs and scale-ups. The initial conversation will help determine whether the format fits your situation.
Do you work internationally?
Yes. Engagements can be conducted in French, English or Spanish. In Paris, remotely, or in person depending on context.
What happens if you say no?
It happens. Klaira won't accept an engagement that doesn't make sense. If it's not the right direction, you'll know — and you'll still walk away with a useful takeaway.
